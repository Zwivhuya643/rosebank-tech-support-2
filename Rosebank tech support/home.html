Rosebank Tech Support

Reliable • Secure • Connected

Rosebank Tech Support was founded in 2020 to offer specialized technical support services to the students, lecturers, and administrative staff of Rosebank College. This department has been put in place to facilitate the provision of technical assistance to all users regarding any issues they face related to IT. These include problems such as password changes, logging into the students' portal, installing software, internet connection, emails, and computer troubleshooting.
